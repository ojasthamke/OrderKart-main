🌾 Our Mission: Farm-Fresh Harvest to Your Doorstep

OrderKart was founded with a simple, powerful conviction: everyone deserves crisp, highly nutritious vegetables and fruits harvested fresh from local fields, delivered without middleman inflation or days of warehouse refrigeration.

Operated under ApliBhaji Fresh Foods, we bridge the gap between hard-working rural farming communities and neighborhood urban kitchens. Every morning, our team curates fresh harvests straight from local farmers and regional APMC wholesale mandis, grades them with stringent hygiene standards, and fulfills deliveries directly to your doorstep in as fast as 37 minutes or through scheduled morning slots.

✨ What Makes OrderKart Different?

🌱 Direct Farm Sourcing

Zero cold storage preservation chemicals. Produce moves from soil to your kitchen within hours of harvest.

⏱️ Hyper-Speed Fulfillment

Dedicated neighborhood road-based delivery logistics ensuring punctual deliveries in Yavatmal.

🤝 Community Bulk Buy

Innovative group buying pools allowing neighborhood families to pool volume and unlock wholesale mandi rates.

🔒 Safe & Transparent Payments

Secure RBI-compliant digital payments via PayU, instant UPI QR transfers, or Cash on Delivery.

🏛️ Company & Legal Background

ApliBhaji Fresh Foods is a registered agri-commerce and retail enterprise founded in Maharashtra, India. We power both consumer mobile applications and comprehensive retail POS ecosystems to ensure farm produce is weighed accurately, priced honestly in Indian Rupees (INR), and delivered with supreme customer satisfaction.
